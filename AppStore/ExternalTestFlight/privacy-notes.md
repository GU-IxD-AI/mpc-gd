# App Privacy Notes

These notes are for filling App Store Connect’s App Privacy questionnaire. Confirm with the study/data owner before final submission.

## Data Collected In Study Mode
The app can send study interaction data to the project server when the user joins the study.

Likely collected data:
- User identifier generated by the app.
- Study demographics entered by the user: age, gender, region.
- Game interaction events.
- Design modifications.
- Game ratings: difficulty, creativity, fun, winnable.
- Gameplay outcome: win/loss, elapsed time, score.
- Game/chromosome design data for the played or modified game.

## Data Not Intentionally Collected
- Precise location.
- Contacts.
- Photos or videos.
- Camera or microphone data.
- Health or financial data.
- Advertising identifier.

## Tracking
Suggested answer: No, the app does not track users across apps or websites for advertising or data-broker purposes.

## Linked To User
Confirm with the research team. The generated user ID and study responses may be linkable within the research dataset.

## Privacy Policy
External TestFlight and App Store submission should provide a Privacy Policy URL explaining:
- what study data is collected,
- why it is collected,
- who operates the server,
- how long it is retained,
- how participants can request deletion or ask questions.

## Network Note
The current server endpoint uses `http://interactions.se:32405` with an ATS exception. Apple reviewers may ask why insecure HTTP is required. Prefer HTTPS before public App Store release if possible.
