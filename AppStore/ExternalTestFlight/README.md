# Paravida External TestFlight Package

This folder collects the text and assets needed to prepare Paravida for external TestFlight review and later App Store submission.

## Included
- `metadata.md`: App Store Connect and TestFlight text fields.
- `screenshot-plan.md`: exact screenshots to capture on device/simulator.
- `privacy-notes.md`: App Privacy and data-use notes to enter in App Store Connect.
- `review/review-notes.md`: Beta App Review notes.
- `icons/paravida-app-icon-1024.png`: existing 1024x1024 marketing icon from the asset catalog.
- `screenshots/`: folders and naming scheme for required captures.

## Important
Apple screenshots should be real device/simulator screenshots of the app. Do not upload mockups that show UI not present in the app.
