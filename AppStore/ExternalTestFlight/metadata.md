## TestFlight Beta Information
Beta App Description:
Paravida lets players explore and test small physics-based games created through a visual game-design interface. External testers are asked to play games, adjust designs, and report crashes, confusing flows, or places where text is hard to read.

What to Test:
1. Join or skip the study flow on first launch.
2. In study mode, play games from StudyPack and complete the post-game rating form.
3. Adjust game designs, especially winning conditions, then press Play and confirm the game still runs.
4. Close and relaunch the app, then complete another game.
5. Check that any on-screen orange diagnostic text is reported back with the exact wording.

## App Store Listing Draft
Promotional Text:
Explore, remix, and test playful physics games through a visual design interface.

Description:
Paravida is an experimental game-design playground. Players can try compact physics-based games, inspect how each game is built, and adjust design parameters such as controllers, goals, scoring, lives, timing, characters, audio, and backgrounds.

The app is also used for research studies on game design and player experience. In study mode, participants can play curated games, change designs, and provide short post-game ratings. Study interactions are cached if the network is unavailable and retried later.

Key features:
- Play a collection of compact physics-based games.
- Explore and modify game rules through a visual design interface.
- Test games immediately after changing them.
- Rate games in study mode after play.
- Offline-tolerant study logging for unreliable connections.
