Background Gradient Proposals

Nine proposed nine-step background families for the existing Paravida convention where one theme is rendered as Name0 through Name8. The card gradient is only a palette overview; generated previews are separate solid-colour files for each stop.

These are inspired by broad cultural colour associations, academic colour theory, research traditions, theological colour symbolism, and civic/scientific visual languages. They are not intended as fixed or universal meanings. Patterns use adjacent palette colours and drift consistently across each family's nine files.
